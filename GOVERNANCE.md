# Governance

The initial maintainer is [WhenWen](https://github.com/WhenWen). All contributors may propose edits and challenge classifications. A maintainer merges contributions after checking the review checklist; CI passing is necessary for structured changes but is not scientific approval.

Candidates may be merged with clearly stated uncertainty. Promotion to a literature-audited or empirically demonstrated gap requires a recorded review, full-text source checking, and a bounded scope. We aim for review by someone other than the author for promotions; if unavailable, record the self-review explicitly rather than implying independent validation.

Disagreements stay visible in the problem card and linked issue. Review scientific claims and scope, not researcher identity. Evidence of an existing solution has the same standing as evidence of a gap. Authors' lack of response is not agreement. Maintainers may request revisions, narrow claims, or defer a status change with a public explanation.

Use issues for project proposals and appeals. Proposed changes to scope, taxonomy, evidence levels or governance should be discussed in a PR before applying them broadly. Contributors may request to become area editors through an issue after sustained reviewing contributions; appointments and scope will be recorded here.

This is a curated, versioned research resource, not a novelty certification service or a journal. Use a commit URL when citing a card so the referenced state is recoverable.
