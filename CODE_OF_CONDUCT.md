# Community conduct

Discuss evidence respectfully. Welcome corrections and participants across career stages. Do not harass, discriminate, disclose personal information, or misrepresent another person's contribution. Critique claims rather than people.

Report non-sensitive concerns in an issue. For sensitive concerns, use the contact route currently published on the maintainer's GitHub profile; do not post private details publicly. Maintainers may remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation, explaining decisions when doing so would not disclose sensitive information.
